KPK Tourism Information

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is a province of Pakistan with many famous
tourist destinations including Swat, Kalam, Malam Jabba, Chitral,
Kumrat, Naran, Kaghan, and the Kalash Valley.

Swat:
Swat is known for mountains, rivers, forests and scenic valleys.
Popular places include Mingora, Saidu Sharif, Kalam and Malam Jabba.

Kalam:
Kalam is a popular tourist destination in Swat known for its
mountains, forests and rivers.

Kumrat:
Kumrat Valley is located in Upper Dir and is famous for forests,
mountains, rivers and waterfalls.

Chitral:
Chitral is known for its mountain scenery, culture and Kalash valleys.

Tourism Tip:
Travelers should check weather conditions, road conditions and
local travel advisories before visiting mountainous areas.
